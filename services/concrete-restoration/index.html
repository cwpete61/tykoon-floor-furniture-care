Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from pressure washing?

Pressure washing only cleans the surface. Our Tykoon Concrete Restoration System removes deep grime, treats stains, and seals your concrete so results actually last.

How long do results last?

Unlike standard cleaning, our process includes protection — helping your concrete stay cleaner longer and resist future staining.

Do you remove oil stains and tire marks?

Yes. We use targeted stain treatments to lift oil stains, tire marks, and deep discoloration that pressure washing can't remove.

How long does the job take?

Most residential driveways, patios, and pool decks are completed in as little as 48 hours from start to sealed. Larger or heavily damaged surfaces may take longer — we give you the timeline up front with your estimate.

Do I need to tear out cracked concrete?

In most cases, no. We repair and blend cracks, chips, and spalling as part of the restoration — no demolition, no pouring, and a fraction of the cost of replacement. If a slab is structurally failing, we'll tell you straight instead of selling you a repair that won't hold.
